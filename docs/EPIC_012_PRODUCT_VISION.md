# EPIC-012 Product Vision

Rewardly's mobile app is the first-party client for the Payment Intelligence Platform. EPIC-012 shifts the first screen from a feature list into a personal payment briefing.

The product promise remains simple: Rewardly helps the user make the smartest payment decision right now.

## Experience Principles

- Anticipate before asking. If the platform already has wallet, plan, journey, coach, or context data, the app should surface the next best action.
- Prefer one confident recommendation over many options.
- Reduce taps to Smart Pay, planning, wallet coach, and recent decisions.
- Keep intelligence in the platform. The app ranks platform outputs; it does not score cards or benefits.
- Keep the experience calm. Avoid dense metrics, dashboards, charts, and competing calls to action.

## Mobile Experience

The home screen now behaves like a daily briefing:

- A top recommendation explains the most useful action.
- Secondary context cards remain available without overwhelming the user.
- Empty states guide the next meaningful step, such as adding a card or starting Smart Pay.
- Nearby merchants, favorites, plans, wallet coach, and payment journey all feel connected.
