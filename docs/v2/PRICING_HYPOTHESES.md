# Pricing Hypotheses

## Options

Monthly subscription: predictable but may not align with decision volume.
Usage based: simple value link but can create adoption friction.
Base plus usage: best long-term fit.
Design-partner pricing: best first step.

## Recommendation

Use design-partner pilots first. Offer sandbox free. For paid pilot, use a modest monthly platform fee with included decision volume and a written success criterion.

Pricing starts when a partner moves from sandbox evaluation to a real user-facing or production-like pilot.

## Willingness-To-Pay Signals

- Partner assigns engineer.
- Partner shares real scenarios.
- Partner asks for live environment.
- Partner measures user behavior or value delivered.
- Partner requests data retention, logs, or support commitments.
