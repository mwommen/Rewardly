# Design Partner Program

## Ideal Partner

Small to mid-stage fintech or AI finance product with wallet/card data, product urgency, one engineer available, and willingness to review decision quality weekly.

## Pilot

Length: 6-8 weeks.

Partner obligations:

- Product owner and engineer.
- Sandbox integration.
- Representative scenarios.
- Weekly feedback.
- Success metric agreement.

Rewardly obligations:

- API support.
- Rule review.
- Decision-quality reports.
- Debugging.
- Weekly review.
- Roadmap transparency.

## Success Metrics

Time to first API call, decision success rate, low-confidence rate, disputed decisions, user interaction rate where available, and partner willingness to move to paid pilot.

## Exit

Exit if partner cannot provide wallet data, cannot assign engineer, or cannot define a measurable use case.
