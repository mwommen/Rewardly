# Competitive Positioning

## Positioning Statement

Rewardly gives financial products an embeddable, wallet-first payment decision engine: one recommended payment method, estimated value, explanation, confidence, alternatives, and audit trace.

## Category Analysis

| Category | What they sell | Overlap | Advantage | Weakness vs Rewardly | Strategy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Consumer card-optimization apps | Consumer app/extension | Recommendations | Existing users | Not infrastructure, customer UX controlled by vendor | Avoid direct consumer fight |
| Card-benefit trackers | Benefit inventory | Benefit data | Focused data UX | Not contextual payment decision | Compete on decisions |
| Card-linked offer platforms | Offers/activation | Offer value | Offer networks | Not wallet-wide deterministic choice | Integrate later |
| Rewards-data APIs | Card reward data | Data | Data breadth | No decision/explanation/audit | Differentiate |
| Credit-card affiliate platforms | Acquisition referrals | Card data | Monetization | Can recommend unowned cards; acquisition-first | Keep separate |
| PFM aggregators | Transaction/account data | Wallet knowledge | Data access | Usually no rule engine | Sell to them |
| Merchant classification providers | MCC/category enrichment | Merchant context | Classification depth | Not payment optimization | Integrate where useful |
| Loyalty infrastructure | Points/rewards programs | Loyalty value | Enterprise integrations | Not owned-wallet card decisions | Avoid initially |
| Payment orchestration | Route payments | Payment execution | Payment rails | Rewardly does not process payments | Avoid/partner |
| Issuer internal tools | Issuer-specific recommendations | Recommendations | Proprietary data | Single issuer bias | Sell later |
| Build-it-yourself | Internal engineering | All | Control | Slow, expensive, brittle | Main competitor |

## Positioning Matrix

Rewardly should occupy:

- Ranked decision over raw data.
- Infrastructure over consumer app.
- Wallet-specific over generic recommendation.
- Explainable over opaque.
- Contextual over static.
- Versioned/auditable over unversioned.

Requires founder validation: named competitor pricing, market size, and buyer urgency.
